The planar Gmsh recipe accepts an optional source-traced target size per layer, no larger than the global target. Interface points use the finer neighboring size, so physical groups and conforming shared facets remain intact. The pinned Gmsh check compares targeted biofilm cell count to the same uniform recipe. This is mesh-resolution evidence, not PDE convergence or a product solver.

P05 manufactured-source follow-up: the pinned native toolchain checks a piecewise-quadratic exact diffusion solution on the same three-layer tagged geometry at factors 1/2/4. On CI run [37618038756](https://github.com/igorbolognani/METREV/actions/runs/37618038756), 92/252/962 cells gave relative L2 errors `1.473e-4`/`6.297e-5`/`1.110e-5` and equivalent-h orders `1.686`/`2.591`. Dirichlet boundary flux is recovered from the unmodified weak residual, including the source load; raw P1 gradient traces remain diagnostics. Relative source-boundary balance stayed below `4e-14`. This synthetic mesh/PDE check does not establish cell physics, experimental accuracy, or product admission; P05 remains partial.

The restricted single-liquid Stokes sidecar now rebuilds the admitted mesh recipe, requires the selected mesh SHA-256 to match, solves with explicit full port tractions, and returns hashed XDMF/HDF5 files whose pressure and x/y velocity dataset paths are checked in the pinned toolchain smoke. Exported P2 velocity is interpolated to P1 to match the first-order mesh; convergence and balance diagnostics use the original Taylor–Hood fields. The separate analytic Poiseuille refinement gate still provides the numerical error and conservation evidence. Both are synthetic development fixtures; no spatial case runner or product solver consumes the outputs.

The isolated scalar finite-volume kernel additionally verifies a first-order linear disappearance term: a manufactured 1D profile refines at three grids; a spatially constant source/loss equilibrium is checked in 1D/2D/3D; transient reaction, storage and boundary flux close the global balance in 1D/2D/3D. These fixtures do not satisfy cell-level reaction, charge or coupled-interface gates.

Prescribed cellwise incompressible velocity is face-averaged on internal finite-volume faces and checked for zero discrete divergence per cell. A synthetic shear field and scalar concentration/source pair refine at three 2D and 3D grid levels, with true solver residual <1e-9 and integrated balance <1e-8 mol/s; a transient constant state remains invariant. This finite-volume fixture uses prescribed velocity; the separate native same-domain development operations transfer solved Stokes/Darcy velocity. No full-cell case runner is admitted.

The reusable tagged-mesh Stokes development component applies full declared traction and no-slip walls, records PETSc convergence, divergence L2, inlet/outlet fluxes and pressure means. The synthetic planar Poiseuille check varies viscosity and pressure drop across three mesh levels and requires relative field and flow errors below 1e-7 and flow imbalance below 1e-8. The component has no product hydraulic admission or porous interface.

The homogeneous porous Darcy fixture solves one tagged biofilm layer with source-traced constant permeability and pore-fluid viscosity. A pinned Gmsh/DOLFINx operation checks the exact linear pressure, uniform Darcy velocity, pressure drop, analytic `Q=(k/μ)(Δp/L)H`, positive inlet/outlet flow, flux balance, divergence and XDMF/HDF5 hashes/dataset paths. The `planar_darcy_transport` fixture uses that solved velocity on the same mesh for one neutral scalar with constant source-traced effective diffusivity and two sourced port concentrations. At `Pe=1`, concentration L2 error is below `2e-3`, each boundary species rate matches the analytic flux within `1e-2`, total integrated species-flux imbalance is below `1e-2`, and all four output datasets are hash checked. The sidecar also reports nodal sample count, min/max, area-weighted mean and integral for each exported scalar field, and identifies the direct PETSc method, iteration count and positive convergence reason for both linear systems; CI checks concentration mean against the analytic profile. The flux tolerance reflects the non-locally-conservative continuous P1 discretization and this fixture resolution; it does not claim the stricter balance of the separate finite-volume kernel. The opt-in development worker adapter maps the sidecar summaries and linear-solver outcomes into result-v2 and stores the verified mesh/HDF5 bytes in owner-scoped local stores. Its worker integration tests include both mocked contracts and pinned native Stokes/Darcy runs through persistence and authenticated field reads. This is not product admission or a production storage provider. Heterogeneous porous media, bulk/porous interface flow, electrochemical/biological reaction networks, ionic migration and cell-run integration remain unverified.

The same kernel verifies a constant, grid-aligned diagonal diffusion tensor through affine fields in 1D/2D/3D and manufactured-source refinement in 2D/3D. It has no off-diagonal tensor terms or unstructured-mesh discretization.

Prescribed outward diffusive-flux boundaries are checked as a no-flux limit with reaction in steady 1D/2D/3D and with implicit transient loss/storage in 1D/2D/3D. Mixed Dirichlet/flux faces and a steady unanchored all-Neumann problem remain unsupported.

Porous-storage extension: heterogeneous time-independent porosity, cellwise production, closed-domain pore-volume-weighted inventory, concentration bounds and implicit linear decay in 1D/2D/3D. Analytic concentration/inventory tolerance 1e-9; integrated step balance tolerance 1e-8. No porosity-derived transport or reactive area.

Stokes-to-species batch: native `spatial_toolchain_smoke.py` transfers solved P2 Poiseuille velocity to a constant tracer on each of the three channel meshes, checking extrema and advective species rates. Native scalar source/loss batch: `spatial_toolchain_smoke.py` checks pure first-order loss, constant production and production/loss equilibrium against analytic profiles at three mesh factors. ## Restricted coupled-cell follow-up

The neutral-partition follow-up executes the reacting MFC and MEC cell with explicit synthetic `K=2` at anode→membrane and `K=1/2` at membrane→cathode. Factors 1/2/3 refine every axis: 18/72/162 cells in 2D and 36/288/972 in 3D. Native mass/charge/circuit balances, finite nonnegative fields and transverse concentration variation are required. Successive current differences must decrease; medium/fine current change is bounded by `1e-3` relative and the volume-weighted concentration change by `5e-3 mol/m3`. A separate `K=1` solve must reproduce every continuous-interface field within `1e-12`; the nonunit fixture must change the concentration field by more than `0.1 mol/m3`, preventing an ignored partition option from passing. The full CLI retains measured observables, exact input/mesh hashes and the current runtime source hash. Interface fluxes are retained as diagnostics, with no flux-convergence claim: uniform transverse reservoirs meet the imposed concentration jump at interface corners. This is a bounded refinement and limiting-case check, without an exact-solution order, charged membrane physics, calibration or independent validation.

The committed v7 refinement record remains immutable historical evidence. Its runtime hash identifies the code used for that execution; current compatibility is checked by replaying the historical inputs and comparing observables. New CLI evidence binds the current runtime bytes rather than relabeling the old record as v8.

Native 2D/3D partition worker cases also reload the immutable input, source-traced coefficient, equation-graph interface law, hashed fields and deterministic development report through the authenticated API. JSON and Markdown reports explicitly list `ideal_neutral_membrane_partition` when selected and continue to mark Donnan equilibrium disabled and decision eligibility false. These memory-repository checks do not establish PostgreSQL deployment, browser admission or independent validation for a general membrane model.

The full numerical regression also executes a separate 3D series with reservoir feeding on `y_min` and `z_min`, refining all three axes at factors 1/2/3 (36/288/972 cells). It measures a nonconstant z-plane concentration mean, all native conservation residuals, finite nonnegative fields, circuit current and volume-weighted concentration. Successive current differences must shrink below 50% of the previous difference; medium/fine current change must remain below `1e-3` relative and concentration-mean change below `1e-3 mol/m3`. Input/mesh hashes and actual observables are retained in the full CLI evidence; the expensive native regression is excluded from fast PR checks. This is a restricted synthetic refinement comparison, with no exact cell solution, experimental validation, transient behavior or general catalog promotion.

`tests/contracts/test_spatial_cell_verification.py` exercises one-sided transverse reservoir feeding with heterogeneous diffusivity and liquid conductivity at 18/72/288 cells. It checks local/global species, liquid/solid charge and MFC load closure, finite nonnegative fields, volume-weighted concentration and current refinement, plus `1e-7`/`1e-9` nonlinear tolerance sensitivity. Successive observable differences must shrink below 40% of the previous difference; medium/fine current differs by less than `2e-5` relative and mean concentration by less than `1e-5 mol/m3`. This is a synthetic restricted-cell check, not an exact solution or an empirical accuracy estimate.

The same suite distinguishes evaluation exhaustion, linear failure, nonfinite step, positivity blockage and line-search failure; no failing candidate is silently accepted or clipped. Repeated solves own independent histories. Native worker tests verify 2D/3D success and 2D failed report/view/mesh/fields. The new cell-specific PostgreSQL and actual-browser gates are required in CI; test existence does not establish gate success. Details and the 1,152-cell budget limit are in the dated verification/product audit. General catalog and independent-validation gates stay pending.
